I · The Compiler Sovereignty Thesis

A theorem is only as true as
the machine instructions it emits.

Conventional research pipelines prove properties of abstract math, then manually rewrite kernels in C++/CUDA and pray that optimizing compilers preserve semantics without introducing register spills or subtle reassociations. HoloEmit makes the compiler itself a Lean 4 proof object—eliminating the translation chasm between OPTIFAB / HoloSphere mathematics and ALETHEION bare-metal silicon.
